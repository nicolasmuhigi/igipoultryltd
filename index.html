Foundation

Founded on a Question. Built on a Standard.

Standing in a supermarket, two young Rwandans, Yvan and Patrick, watched a customer buy eggs that were simply placed into a thin paper bag. As she lifted the bag to put it into her cart, some of the eggs cracked.

We asked ourselves:

“Isn’t there a better way to package eggs in Rwanda?”

That question led to research that later became IGI POULTRY.

Today, we are transforming Rwanda's layer poultry industry through reliable production, hygienic packaging, and full traceability. Every egg we sell is graded, labeled, and traceable either sourced from our own farm or trusted partner farms that meet our standards.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are you importing eggs from somewhere else?

We're 100% Rwandan, started after spotting a gap in how eggs were being sold (loose in paper bags!). Everything is produced and packaged locally.

Can I just buy a small pack of eggs from you, or do you only sell in bulk?

Right now we sell in packs of 6 and 10, and trays of 30, mostly to shops, restaurants, and hotels. The 6-egg pack is great for smaller households too.

How do I know your eggs are actually clean and safe to eat?

Every egg is graded, labeled, and traceable back to the farm it came from, ours or one of our partner farms, all of which follow eco-friendly practices. Nothing is treated with harmful chemicals.

I run a small shop, is my order too small for you to work with?

Not at all. We already supply a wide range of businesses, from big names like SPAR and Carrefour Mart down to neighborhood spots like bakeries and butcheries. We're set up to handle different order sizes.

Do you do anything with all the waste from your chickens?

we turn poultry waste into organic fertilizer, a chemical-free alternative that's great for farmers and cooperatives looking for a more natural way to enrich their soil.

Are you planning to grow beyond just fresh eggs?

Definitely. We're developing egg powder for longer shelf life and export markets, and down the line we want to add liquid eggs, salted eggs, and egg protein powder, plus offer consulting to help other poultry farmers run more sustainable operations.
